# 1.1 Optional Reflection: Build a Sandbox for Your Coding Agent

## Instructions

In this optional activity, you will reflect on the decisions you made during Exercise 2: Build a Sandbox for Your Coding Agent.

If you have worked professionally with production codebases, CI/CD pipelines, or security-sensitive environments, you likely made these decisions from experience and may not need this reflection. Feel free to skip it.

If you are newer to production software development, this reflection is worth your time. The exercise asked you to make real security and access decisions: about what the agent can see, what persists, and whether it worked correctly. Those decisions have consequences that are easy to miss when you are focused on getting the setup to run. The goal here is to slow down and examine whether you understood the choices you made, not just whether you completed the steps.

Writing your responses down rather than just thinking through them will help you get more out of the exercise. When you write out an explanation in your own words, you are retrieving what you know and exposing the places where your understanding has gaps. The act of producing an explanation is what moves knowledge from something you've encountered to something you actually own.

In practice, if you find yourself writing a vague answer, that vagueness is useful information: it tells you that you followed a step without internalizing the reason behind it, and it is a signal to go back to the lesson material and look for the reasoning you may have skimmed past the first time. In that way, you can use a vague answer as a starting point for filling in the gaps in your understanding.
